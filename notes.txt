  - Store updated price entry even when no change in price is detected once every 24 hours. this is to ensure that the price history is up to date and can be used for analytics and reporting.

  ```
  Store if
  price changed
  OR
  24 hours elapsed
  OR
  product became available
  OR
  product went out of stock
  ```

  - Create a dictionary of keywords for each product type and category. For example, for computer components, we can have keywords like "CPU", "GPU", "RAM", "Motherboard", etc. For clothing, we can have keywords like "Shirt", "Pants", "Dress", etc.
  - And then we can track if a product is categorized during the addition to the db, If the fuzzyfind algo failed to categorize the product, we can then update the algo with the new keywords and re-categorize the uncategorized products using scripts. This way we can improve the accuracy of the categorization over time.
  - Implement a way to identify the product type and category and increase/decrease the frequency at which we scrape the product
  Ex:
    For Computer components, pheriperals, and electronics, we can scrape every 5-10 minutes, as the prices change frequently.
    For clothing, shoes, and accessories, we can scrape every 1-2 hours,
    For books, we can scrape every 6-12 hours.


3. Scraping frequency based on price change history
  - Implement a way to identify the price change history of a product and increase/decrease the frequency at which we scrape the product
